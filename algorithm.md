---
  Entry (BUY)

  The bot only buys when two things are true at the same time:
  1. Uptrend confirmed — the 50-day average price is above the 200-day average price (the classic "Golden Cross")
  2. Short-term pullback — RSI is below 35-40 (depending on the coin), meaning the coin has dipped recently and isn't being chased at a high

  The idea: only buy coins that are trending up long-term, but wait for a temporary dip to get a better entry price.

  ---
  Exit (SELL) — whichever hits first:

  1. Take profit — RSI climbs above 60-65, meaning the coin is now overbought/extended. Lock in gains.
  2. Stop loss — price drops 4-8% below your entry price. Cut the loss before it gets worse.
  3. Trend reversal — the 50-day average crosses back below the 200-day average. The uptrend is over, get out.

  ---
  What it doesn't do

  - No leverage, no shorting — spot only
  - No position sizing based on volatility — fixed $20 per trade
  - Only trades once per day (at the daily candle close)
  - Only holds one position per coin at a time

  ---
  The core bet is that buying pullbacks within confirmed uptrends, and cutting losers quickly, produces more winners than losers over time. It won't catch
  every move, and most days it does nothing — that's by design.
